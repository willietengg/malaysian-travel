# Vietnam 🇻🇳 (VN · VND)

*Last reviewed 2026-11. (verify) = re-check before stating.*

## Entry for Malaysian passport holders
- Visa-free for tourism (ASEAN, typically 30 days; verify).
- Passport should have 6+ months' validity.

## Money
- Prices have many zeros (RM 1 ≈ several thousand VND), so always show the RM conversion. Watch out for ₫10,000 vs ₫100,000 note mix-ups.
- Cash is important for street food and markets. Grab can be paid by card.
- Bargain at markets (Ben Thanh, Dong Xuan). Fixed prices in malls.

## Plug & power
Type A/C (two round or flat pins), 220V. Bring an adapter.

## Getting around
Grab (car or bike) everywhere. Domestic flights are cheap for north ↔ south. Sleeper buses and trains run to Sapa, Ninh Binh and Ha Long (cruise).

## Closed days & timing traps
- **Tet (Lunar New Year)**: many shops close for about 5 days, prices rise, and transport is packed.
- Some museums close Monday or for a midday break (about 11:30–13:30).
- **Weather differs by region:** north is cold Dec–Feb (Hanoi ~10–18°C, Sapa can be near 0°C); central (Da Nang, Hoi An) has rain and typhoons Sep–Dec; south is hot all year with a rainy season May–Oct.

## Must-eat (diet notes)
| Dish | Diet note |
|---|---|
| Pho bo / pho ga | Beef / chicken; usually no pork in broth, but check |
| Banh mi | Often pork pâté and cold cuts; ask for chicken, egg or beef |
| Bun cha, com tam, banh xeo | Usually pork |
| Cao lau, mi quang (Hoi An) | Usually pork / shrimp |
| Egg coffee, ca phe sua da | Hanoi café culture |
| Fresh spring rolls (goi cuon) | Shrimp + pork usually; veg versions exist |

Halal: limited. In HCMC, the **Dong Du Street mosque area** (District 1) has halal eateries, and Hanoi has a few around Hang Luoc. Many vegetarian (chay) restaurants serve no-pork travellers.

## Malaysian-specific tips
- Very good value: budget trips are possible at low RM cost.
- Hoi An tailor-made clothes (allow 24–48h), coffee beans, cashews and lacquerware are good buys.
- Agree the price before getting a cyclo or motorbike taxi. Use Grab when possible.
