# Thailand 🇹🇭 (TH · THB)

*Last reviewed 2026-11. (verify) = re-check before stating.*

## Entry for Malaysian passport holders
- Visa-free for tourism (ASEAN, typically 30 days; verify current length).
- **TDAC (Thailand Digital Arrival Card)**: mandatory online form since May 2025. Submit within 3 days before arrival (free, on the official site only).
- Overland options from northern Malaysia: Bukit Kayu Hitam ↔ Sadao, and the Padang Besar train.

## Money
- Cash is king for street food and markets. Thai ATMs charge a fixed fee (about ฿200+) per withdrawal, so exchange in MY or use a multi-currency card.
- **Pay by QR from Malaysian banking apps**: DuitNow cross-border QR is linked with Thai PromptPay at many merchants (check that the user's bank supports it).
- VAT refund for tourists: minimum ฿2,000 per shop per day, with a total minimum (verify). Claim at the airport.

## Plug & power
Mixed sockets (A/B/C/O), 220V. Many take two-pin plugs. Bring a universal adapter.

## Getting around
BTS/MRT in Bangkok, Grab or Bolt for rides, river boats (Chao Phraya Express). Avoid unmetered tuk-tuk "tours".

## Closed days & timing traps
- **Grand Palace** opens daily but closes for royal ceremonies. Strict dress code: covered shoulders and knees.
- **Chatuchak**: the full market runs **Sat–Sun**; the plant section runs Wed–Thu.
- Many museums close Monday. Some Buddhist holidays have **alcohol sale bans**.
- **Songkran** (13–15 Apr): water fights everywhere. Fun, but phones get wet and prices go up.

## Must-eat (diet notes)
| Dish | Diet note |
|---|---|
| Tom yum, pad thai, som tam | Usually no pork, but fish sauce and shrimp paste are common |
| Khao man gai | Chicken (halal versions exist) |
| Boat noodles, moo ping, khao kha moo | Pork or blood-based: avoid for halal / no-pork |
| Khao soi (Chiang Mai) | Chicken or beef versions; many halal shops in Chiang Mai's Muslim quarter |
| Mango sticky rice, Thai milk tea | Vegetarian-friendly |

Halal: Thailand has a big Muslim community. Look for the **Thai halal logo (CICOT)**. Southern Thailand (Hat Yai) is easy for halal food. Bangkok has halal options around Ramkhamhaeng, the Pratunam mosque area and many malls.

## Malaysian-specific tips
- Short flights mean a 3-day weekend works. Low-leave trips are Thailand's strength.
- Thai 7-Eleven snacks, Naraya bags, Thai cosmetics and pharmacy items are popular buys not found in MY.
- Nov–Feb is the cool, dry high season. Jun–Oct (rain) is cheapest.
