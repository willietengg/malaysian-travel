# Singapore 🇸🇬 (SG · SGD)

*Last reviewed 2026-11. (verify) = re-check before stating.*

## Entry for Malaysian passport holders
- Visa-free for short visits, typically up to 30 days per entry. Passport should have 6+ months' validity (verify on ICA).
- **SG Arrival Card (SGAC)** is required for every entry by air, land or sea. Submit it **free** on the ICA website or **MyICA app** within 3 days before arrival (the arrival day counts). Paid third-party "SGAC" sites are not endorsed by ICA. Avoid them.
- **Driving in:** since 6 Oct 2026, a foreign-registered car must have a **valid VEP** before its occupants can submit the SGAC. The system checks the car's VEP status (Woodlands and Tuas first, other vehicle types later).
- Land checkpoints use **QR-code clearance** (MyICA app) instead of showing a passport. First-time foreign visitors, or anyone using a new passport, still go to a counter once so their biometrics can be enrolled (verify current rules).
- Vapes / e-cigarettes are illegal in SG and enforced hard. No duty-free allowance for cigarettes. Don't bring chewing gum to sell or in bulk.

## Money
- SGD. Cards and contactless work almost everywhere, hawker centres included (many take SGQR / PayNow / NETS).
- **Pay with Malaysian bank apps:** DuitNow cross-border QR lets you scan **SGQR / NETS QR** at participating merchants (live since Mar 2023). DuitNow ↔ **PayNow** person-to-person transfers by phone number have been live since Nov 2023. Daily caps and supported banks differ: check the user's bank app. FOMO Pay announced **DuitNow QR acceptance** at SG merchants ahead of the RTS Link (Jun 2026; rollout status verify).
- **GST tourist refund (eTRS):** GST 9%. Minimum S$100 (incl. GST) at one retailer on the same day (up to 3 receipts). Claim at the eTRS kiosk at **Changi or Seletar Airport only**, within 2 months of purchase. **No refund if you leave by the Causeway / bus / car.**
- Multi-currency cards (Wise, BigPay, etc.) beat bank FX for SGD.

## Plug & power
Type G (three square pins), 230V. **Same as Malaysia. No adapter needed.**

## Getting around
- MRT + buses: tap a contactless Visa/Mastercard/Amex or a mobile wallet (SimplyGo). Foreign Visa/Mastercard cards pay a small daily admin fee (about S$0.60 per day of use, verify). Amex has no fee. Use the same card to tap in and out.
- Ride-hailing: Grab, Gojek, TADA, CDG Zig. Google Maps and Citymapper both work well.
- Changi: Jewel is open to the public (no flight needed). Arrive 2–3h early on peak weekends.

## Closed days & timing traps
- **Gardens by the Bay** Flower Dome and Cloud Forest each close about **one day a month for maintenance** (different days). Check the official calendar before you go.
- **Hawker centres** close for cleaning a few days at a time (often quarterly), and stalls often take one weekday off. Check before you plan a meal around one stall.
- **Chinese New Year** (6–8 Feb 2027): many hawker stalls and small shops close for the first 2–3 days. Chinatown is packed in the week before.
- **USS** rides close for refurbishment in rotation (e.g. Transformers, WaterWorld). Check the "attraction closures" page. Book dated tickets online. Price depends on the date (verify).
- **Causeway jams:** worst on Fri evening/Sat morning (SG→JB) and Sun evening (JB→SG), and on the eve of SG or MY long weekends and school holidays. Use the MyICA app or the Checkpoint.sg traffic cams.
- **F1 Singapore GP 8–10 Oct 2027:** Marina Bay hotels spike and roads close. Big concerts at the National Stadium have the same effect.

Halal: **MUIS** certifies. Search its directory or the **MuslimSG app**. Since Oct 2025, certificates carry a QR code you can scan to check they're valid. Certification is voluntary, so "Muslim-owned" ≠ certified. Mosques: Sultan Mosque (Kampong Glam), and many neighbourhood mosques.

## Malaysian-specific tips
- At ~RM3+ per SGD, everything feels expensive. Hawker meals are the value play. Many Malaysians sleep in JB and cross daily; factor in border queues and the last bus/train.
- What Malaysians actually go for: **concerts** (Asia-exclusive shows), **USS / Sentosa**, Jewel, Gardens by the Bay, shopping sales (Great Singapore Sale, year-end).
- Already in MY, so lower priority: Ya Kun, Toast Box, Charles & Keith, Pedro, Uniqlo, Muji, DON DON DONKI, Sephora. SG-only souvenirs (Bengawan Solo, Irvins salted-egg chips, Bacha Coffee): check brands-in-malaysia.md (verify).
- NE monsoon (Dec–Jan) brings heavy afternoon showers. Bring an umbrella, not a jacket.
