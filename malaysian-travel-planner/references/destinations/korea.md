# South Korea 🇰🇷 (KR · KRW)

*Last reviewed 2026-11. (verify) = re-check before stating.*

## Entry for Malaysian passport holders
- Visa-free short stay (generally up to 90 days).
- **K-ETA is required for Malaysians.** Malaysia is not on Korea's temporary K-ETA exemption list, which was extended to 31 Dec 2026 (verify on eta.go.kr). Fee is about ₩10,000. Apply at least 72h before departure, and only via the official site or app.
- An e-Arrival Card is also in use. Travellers with an approved K-ETA may be exempt (verify).

## Money
- Cards work almost everywhere, including taxis and convenience stores. Keep some cash for markets.
- T-money (transport) or a tourist card like WOWPASS (prepaid card + transport).
- Tax refund is often instant at the counter at participating shops (minimum spend applies; verify the current threshold).

## Plug & power
Type C/F (two round pins), 220V. Malaysian devices are fine, but you need an adapter.

## Getting around
- **Use Naver Map or KakaoMap**: Google Maps walking and driving directions are weak in Korea. Use Papago to translate and Kakao T for taxis.
- KTX high-speed rail Seoul ↔ Busan takes about 2.5h.

## Closed days & timing traps
- **Gyeongbokgung closes Tuesday**; **Changdeokgung and Deoksugung close Monday**. Many museums close Monday.
- **Seollal** (Lunar New Year) and **Chuseok**: many shops and restaurants close, and transport sells out.
- Popular cafés, pop-ups in Seongsu and K-pop experiences often need online booking or queue apps.

## Must-eat (diet notes)
| Dish | Diet note |
|---|---|
| Korean BBQ | Samgyeopsal = pork; hanu beef is pricey but great |
| Korean fried chicken | Not halal unless certified; halal chicken shops exist in Itaewon |
| Tteokbokki, kimbap, odeng | Fish cakes; broths may contain pork or anchovy |
| Ganjang gejang (soy crab) | No pork |
| Bibimbap, naengmyeon, samgyetang | Samgyetang is chicken (not halal unless certified) |
| Myeongdong street food | Mixed |

Halal: the **Itaewon** mosque area has many halal restaurants. The Korea Tourism Organization classifies Muslim-friendly restaurants.

## Malaysian-specific tips
- Olive Young in MY is only a small Sephora corner plus Shopee, so the Korean flagship stores are `must` for beauty fans.
- Daiso Korea is different from Japanese Daiso and very cheap.
- Winter is often below 0°C, Seoul can hit −10°C. Spring (Apr) and autumn (Oct–Nov) are the sweet spots.
